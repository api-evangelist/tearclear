# TearClear

> TearClear Corp. is a clinical-stage ophthalmic pharmaceutical company. It develops a
> preservative-removing drug delivery platform — a chemical filter built into a conventional
> multi-dose eye-drop bottle that captures benzalkonium chloride (BAK) at the point of
> instillation — so a medication stays preserved in the bottle but reaches the ocular surface
> preservative-free. TearClear ships drug products and delivery hardware, not software.

## What TearClear is

- Founded 2015. Clinical-stage ophthalmic pharmaceutical company.
- Platform: a porous oxidized-polyethylene (OxPO / OxHDPE) filter integrated into the bottle
  tip, which strips benzalkonium chloride from the drop as it is dispensed.
- Lead candidate: TC-002 (latanoprost ophthalmic solution 0.005%), which met the primary and
  all secondary endpoints in the CLEAR Phase 3 pivotal glaucoma trial.
- Pipeline behind it: TC-001 (timolol), TC-003 (brimonidine/timolol), TC-004 (brimonidine).
- Privately held; funded through later-stage venture rounds. Its shares appear on secondary
  marketplaces, which is how this company entered the API Evangelist harvest backlog.
